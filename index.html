about elfboi

elfboi is a solo studio intent on making accessible, utility apps with fun & art in mind. Often collaborating with other artists & makers in the space. Software that solves a single problem, looks good doing it, and asks little of the people using it.

The work sits at the intersection of design and craft, built by hands trained in art and sound. Every app is approached with intention, restraint, and a point of view!

For art, music, and other less utilitarian work, see leviathe.studio.
